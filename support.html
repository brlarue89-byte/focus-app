Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a task?

Tap the "+" button on the Tasks tab to add a new task for today.

How do I set up repeating tasks?

Tap the repeat icon (↺) on any task, choose your frequency (daily, weekdays, weekends, or weekly), and optionally set a time.

How does the streak work?

Complete at least one task every day to keep your streak alive. Missing a day resets it — unless you use your streak shield!

What is the Focus Timer?

The Focus Timer is a Pomodoro-style timer. Choose 25 or 50 minutes, focus on your task, and earn XP when you complete a session.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to the Account tab and tap "Cancel subscription." You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Account tab and tap "Delete account." This permanently removes all your data and cannot be undone.

My tasks aren't saving — what do I do?

Try signing out and back in. If the issue continues, contact us and we'll help resolve it.
